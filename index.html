FAQ
Is this free to use?
Absolutely. Ahead is 100% free for all students on campus.
Do I need an account?
No! We use session-based smart ordering so you can skip the sign-up.
How does the vendor know my order?
Our real-time cloud server syncs your order instantly to the vendor dashboard.
Can I cancel an order?
Orders can be canceled up until the vendor starts preparing them.
How is my data handled?
Your data stays safe locally in your browser and on our secure cloud base.
What happens if a vendor is closed?
The app automatically updates vendor status, so you can't place orders when they're closed.
